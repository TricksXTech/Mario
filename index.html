ROTATE TO LANDSCAPE

Landscape mode is mandatory on phones, tablets, and iPads. Turn your device sideways to start playing.

Game will start automatically once your screen is in landscape.

GAME GUIDE

Keyboard Controls

  • ← →Move Left / Right
  • ↑ or SpaceJump & Stomp Enemies
  • ↓Crouch
  • Shift / Ctrl / ASprint & Shoot Fireballs (Fire Mario)
  • H or EscOpen / Close Help (Pauses Game)
  • PPause / Resume Game

Touch Controls (Mobile / Tablet / iPad)

  • ◀ ▶ / ▼Slide or Tap to Move & Crouch
  • A JUMPJump & Stomp Enemies
  • B RUN / FIREHold to Sprint • Tap to Shoot Fireballs
  • PAUSE / ? HELPTop Center Buttons to Pause or View Guide

Power-Ups & Items

  • Coins: Collect 100 coins to earn an extra life (+1 UP).
  • Mushroom (? Box): Grow into Big Mario to survive a hit.
  • Fire Flower (? Box when Big): Throw bouncing fireballs with Sprint/Fire.
  • Star (? Box): Temporary invincibility — defeat enemies on contact.

Gameplay Tips

  • Stomp Goombas and Green Turtles from above; kick shells to clear enemies ahead.
  • Avoid Spiked Turtles and Piranha Plants — use Fireballs or a Star instead.
  • Reach the far right of each stage to advance to the next level.
